About Me

Personality & Perspective

The way I approach school and software development is influenced by my values, my interests and the people who have helped guide me.

My Strongest Traits

Honest

I value being truthful about what I know, what I do not know and the quality of the work I produce. In school this helps me take responsibility for mistakes, ask questions when I need help and learn from feedback instead of pretending that I understand something.

Loyal

I take the people and teams I work with seriously. Loyalty encourages me to be dependable in group assignments, support my teammates and complete the part of the work that others are relying on me to do.

Hardworking

I am willing to spend time solving problems and improving work until I am satisfied with the result. This is especially important in Computer Science because programming often requires patience, debugging and repeated attempts before a solution works properly.

Introverted: I am also naturally introverted. I tend to observe and think before I speak, which helps me concentrate when studying or programming. At the same time, working on development teams has helped me become more comfortable communicating ideas and collaborating with others.

My Hobbies

Gaming

Gaming is one of the ways I relax. It also keeps me interested in technology and makes me appreciate the amount of design, logic and problem-solving that goes into creating interactive software.

Going to the Gym

Training in the gym gives me a routine outside of school and technology. It requires consistency and discipline, which are habits that also help me stay committed to academic and development goals.

Swimming

Swimming gives me a chance to disconnect from screens, clear my mind and stay active. Having activities outside of school helps me return to my work with better focus.

My Mentor: Oldane Graham

One of the people who has had an important impact on my development in technology is Oldane Graham. He helped introduce me to the technology space and taught me how to work as part of a development team using Agile practices.

Oldane works in areas such as digital transformation, software consulting, project management, Agile and Scrum. His professional work and writing focus strongly on helping teams organize their work, collaborate and deliver useful technology solutions.

What stands out to me most is that the lessons were practical. Learning how developers communicate, work through tasks together and operate within an Agile environment gave me experience that goes beyond simply knowing how to code.

I also had the opportunity to create Oldane's personal website. Building a website for someone who helped guide me into the field made the project especially meaningful and allowed me to apply the development skills I was learning.

Personal takeaway: Technical ability matters, but being able to work with people, communicate progress and contribute to a team is just as important.
